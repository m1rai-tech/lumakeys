# Audio Sample Licenses

## Steinway B

93 recordings from the **Versilian Community Sample Library**. The app uses three dynamic layers across 31 pitches, selected from the **StaPiano** AAC collection and embedded in `steinway-b.js`. The recordings are released under CC0 1.0.

- [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://versilian-studios.com/vcsl/)
- [StaPiano sample distribution](https://github.com/StanislavMakhnytskyi/stapiano-samples/tree/main/steinway-b)
- [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

## Yamaha C5

30 recordings from **Salamander Grand Piano** by **Alexander Holm**. The MP3 recordings from the Tone.js audio collection are embedded in `yamaha-c5.js`. The app pitch-shifts the nearest sampled notes to cover the notes between them.

- [Salamander Grand Piano](https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander/)
- [Tone.js audio distribution](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander)
- [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
